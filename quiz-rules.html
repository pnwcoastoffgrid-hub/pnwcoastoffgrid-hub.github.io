Weekly Chat Quiz – Official Rules

Effective date: November 9, 2026

1. Overview and sponsor

The Weekly Chat Quiz is a free, skill-based trivia contest held in the live chat of the PNW Coast Off-Grid YouTube channel (@PNWCoastOff-Grid). The channel's AI chat assistant, Bleep Blorp Bot, asks the questions and keeps score. The top three scorers each week win cash prizes.

The sponsor is PNW Coast Off-Grid ("Sponsor"), contact pnwcoastoffgrid@gmail.com.

No purchase or payment of any kind is necessary to enter or win. Channel memberships, Super Chats, donations, likes and subscriptions do not earn points or improve your chance of winning. Winners are decided by skill, not chance.

2. Who can play

The quiz is open to legal residents of the 50 United States and the District of Columbia who are 18 or older.

These people may play for fun but cannot score points or win prizes:

  • Moderators of the PNW Coast Off-Grid channel
  • The Sponsor and members of the Sponsor's household
  • Members of a moderator's household

You must use your own YouTube account. One account per person. Sponsor may ask a winner to confirm their age and state of residence before paying a prize.

3. Weekly contest period

Each contest week starts Sunday at 7:00:00 PM Pacific Time and ends the following Sunday at 6:59:59 PM Pacific Time. Points reset to zero at the start of each week.

The Sponsor's clock (the timestamp YouTube gives each chat message) is the official time for every question, answer and deadline.

First prize week: the first cash-prize week starts at 7:00 PM Pacific Time on the first Sunday after these rules are published. Quiz questions before then are play-for-fun only and earn no prizes.

4. How to play

  1. Bleep Blorp Bot posts the same quiz question in the live chat of every running PNW Coast Off-Grid live stream about every 15 minutes.
  2. Type your answer in the live chat before the bot posts the answer, about 5 minutes after the question.
  3. Only your first answer to each question counts. Later messages, edits and repeat guesses don't.
  4. Each question counts once: only your first answer on any stream earns a point. Points are totaled under your YouTube account.

Questions cover prediction markets, trading concepts, building bots with AI tools, the channel and general knowledge. Questions are for fun and education only and are not financial advice. Questions are posted in English, and answers must be in English.

If a stream is offline or a question isn't posted for any reason, no points are available for that slot.

5. Scoring, judging and ties

Each correct answer earns 1 point. Wrong or late answers earn nothing and cost nothing.

Bleep Blorp Bot judges answers and accepts reasonable variations in spelling and wording. Every question, answer and point is logged. If you think an answer was judged wrong, email the Sponsor before the week's winners are announced. The Sponsor reviews the log and may correct points. The Sponsor's decision is final.

You need at least 1 point to win. Ties split the prize: if players tie for a place, the prizes for the places they cover are added together and split equally, rounded to the cent. For example, if two players tie for 1st, they split the 1st and 2nd prizes ($15 each) and the next player wins 3rd.

Top-5 players may receive on-stream recognition, such as titles in chat greetings. There are no prizes beyond the cash prizes listed above.

7. Claiming your prize

To claim, email pnwcoastoffgrid@gmail.com with:

  • your YouTube handle, and
  • your PayPal, Venmo or Cash App handle or payment link.

You must claim by the following Sunday at 7:00 PM Pacific Time, one week after the announcement. Unclaimed prizes are forfeited and are not awarded to anyone else. Sponsor pays prizes by PayPal, Venmo or Cash App, normally within 7 days of a valid claim. Sponsor is not responsible for payments sent to a wrong handle provided by the winner.

8. Fair play and disqualification

Sponsor may remove points from, disqualify, or withhold a prize from anyone who:

  • uses more than one account, or bots, scripts or automated tools to answer
  • shares answers with other players to coordinate scores, or otherwise cheats
  • breaks the YouTube Community Guidelines or the channel's chat rules
  • harasses other players, the moderators or the Sponsor

Messages removed by moderators do not count.

YouTube is not a sponsor of this contest and is not involved in running it. By playing, you release YouTube (Google LLC) from any liability related to the quiz.

All chat messages must follow the YouTube Community Guidelines. Answers that don't follow them are disqualified.

To run the quiz, Sponsor collects your YouTube handle and your public chat answers, and from winners, their email address and payment handle. This information is used only to score the quiz, announce winners, pay prizes and keep business and tax records. It is never sold or shared, except with the payment app used to send your prize.
