Privacy Policy for sevenstreams-247 and bleepblorp-chat

Effective date: September 29, 2026

sevenstreams-247 and bleepblorp-chat are private tools operated by the owner of the PNW Coast Off-Grid YouTube channel. sevenstreams-247 is used only to manage that channel's own live streams. bleepblorp-chat reads public live chat and posts messages as Bleep Blorp Bot.

What the apps access

With the channel owner's permission, sevenstreams-247 and bleepblorp-chat use the YouTube Data API. sevenstreams-247 manages the owner's live broadcasts: creating and ending broadcasts, updating titles, descriptions, thumbnails, and categories, inserting ad breaks, and reading and posting live chat messages on the owner's streams. bleepblorp-chat reads public live chat messages on the channel's streams and posts replies as the Bleep Blorp Bot channel.

What the apps do not do

They do not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties. They do not use data for advertising or profiling. They are not offered to the public, and no other users sign in to them.

Data storage

Authorization tokens are stored only on the channel owner's private server, with access restricted to the owner. The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Public chat messages

When sevenstreams-247 or bleepblorp-chat reads live chat on the owner's streams, it processes public messages that viewers post in that chat. Any notes kept about chat participants stay on the owner's private server and are never sold or shared. Chat participants can ask Bleep Blorp Bot to forget them with "!forgetme", and stored notes are then deleted.

Google API Services

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs by sevenstreams-247 and bleepblorp-chat adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
