Weekly Paper Trading PNL Challenge – Official Rules

Effective date: November 13, 2026

1. Overview and sponsor

The Weekly Paper Trading PNL Challenge is a free weekly contest on the PNW Coast Off-Grid YouTube channel (@PNWCoastOff-Grid). Players submit a trading strategy, the channel's bot paper-trades it against live market prices for the rest of the week, and the strategies with the highest net paper profit win cash prizes.

There are two separate challenges each week, each with its own prizes:

  • Kalshi Challenge: Kalshi 15-minute crypto and commodities markets.
  • Polymarket Challenge: Polymarket US 5-minute and 15-minute crypto and commodities markets.

This is paper trading only. No real money is traded, no player money is ever at risk, and you do not need a Kalshi or Polymarket account to play.

The sponsor is PNW Coast Off-Grid ("Sponsor"), contact pnwcoastoffgrid@gmail.com.

No purchase or payment of any kind is necessary to enter or win. Channel memberships, Super Chats, donations, likes and subscriptions do not improve your score or your chance of winning.

2. Who can play

The challenges are open to legal residents of the 50 United States and the District of Columbia who are 18 or older.

These people cannot enter for prizes:

  • Moderators of the PNW Coast Off-Grid channel
  • The Sponsor and members of the Sponsor's household
  • Members of a moderator's household

You must use your own YouTube account. One person, one YouTube channel. Sponsor may ask a winner to confirm their age and state of residence before paying a prize.

3. Weekly contest period

Each challenge week starts Sunday at 7:00:00 PM Pacific Time and ends the following Sunday at 6:59:59 PM Pacific Time. Sponsor's system clock is the official time for every submission, trade and deadline.

First week: the first challenge week runs from Sunday, November 15, 2026, 7:00:00 PM Pacific Time through Sunday, November 22, 2026, 6:59:59 PM Pacific Time. The first winners are announced Sunday, November 22, 2026 at 7:00 PM Pacific Time.

4. How to enter

  1. Submit a strategy at any time during the week, using the submission method announced on the live stream and in the stream description.
  2. You may enter one strategy per challenge per week: one for the Kalshi Challenge and one for the Polymarket Challenge. Each entry is tied to one YouTube channel.
  3. A strategy is locked once submitted and can't be changed or replaced that week.
  4. A strategy identical to one already submitted in the same challenge and week is rejected. The earlier submission keeps its place.

Sponsor may reject a strategy that can't be run by the paper-trading bot, that breaks these rules, or that contains offensive content. A rejected entrant may submit a different strategy that week, if time allows.

5. How strategies are traded and scored

  • Paper trading: once locked, your strategy is paper-traded by Sponsor's bot using live market prices from the relevant platform, from the time it is locked until the week ends. There is no adjustment for strategies submitted later in the week.
  • Position size: every entry is a fixed 10 contracts.
  • Fees: each simulated trade is charged the platform's published trading fees, as calculated by Sponsor's bot.
  • Martingale strategies may use up to 5 stages.
  • Runs: a run is one complete pass through your strategy. For a single-entry strategy, each entry is one run. For a Martingale strategy, a run ends with a win at any stage or a loss at the final stage, and the whole run counts as one result.
  • Minimum activity: a strategy must complete at least 20 runs during the week to be ranked. Runs still open when the week ends are not counted.
  • Score: net paper profit or loss in dollars, after fees, across all completed runs. Highest score wins.

Sponsor's paper-trading records are the official record. If Sponsor finds a simulation error, it may correct scores before winners are announced. Questions about scoring go to the Sponsor by email before the week's winners are announced. The Sponsor's decision is final.

Ties split the prize: if strategies tie for a place, the prizes for the places they cover are added together and split equally, rounded to the cent. For example, if two strategies tie for 1st, they split the 1st and 2nd prizes ($15 each) and the next strategy wins 3rd.
